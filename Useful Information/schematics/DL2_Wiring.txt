Dragon's Lair II Wiring:

J14: (SPEAKER)  pin 1 - LEFT SPEAKER
                pin 2 - LEFT SPEAKER RETURN
                pin 3 - N/C
                pin 4 - N/C
                pin 5 - RIGHT SPEAKER
                pin 6 - RIGHT SPEAKER RETURN
                pin 7 - N/C
                pin 8 - N/C

J15: (COIN)     pin 1 - +5V
                pin 2 - COIN #1
                pin 3 - COIN #2
                pin 4 - N/C
                pin 5 - N/C
                pin 6 - GND

J17: (INPUTS)   pin 1 - +5V
                pin 2 - JOYSTICK UP
                pin 3 - JOYSTICK DOWN
                pin 4 - JOYSTICK LEFT
                pin 5 - JOYSTICK RIGHT
                pin 6 - PLAYER #1 (BUTTON)
                pin 7 - PLAYER #2 (BUTTON)
                pin 8 - SWORD (BOTH BUTTONS)
                pin 9 - SERVICE BUTTON
                pin10 - GND

J1: (POWER)     pin 1 - N/C
                pin 2 - +5V
                pin 3 - +12V
                pin 4 - N/C
                pin 5 - GND
                pin 6 - GND
                pin 7 - GND
                pin 8 - GND
                pin 9 - -5V
                pin10 - +5V
                pin11 - +5V
                pin12 - +5V
 
J2: (POWER)     pin 1 - +12V
                pin 2 - +12V
                pin 3 - GND
                pin 4 - GND

P3: (RS232C)    pin 1 - GND
                pin 2 - TxD (TRANSMIT DATA) 
                pin 3 - RxD (RECEIVE DATA)
                pin 4 - RTS (REQUEST TO SEND)
                pin 5 - CTS (CLEAR TO SEND)
                pin 6 - DSR (DATA SET READY)
                pin 7 - GND
                pin 8 - DCD
                pin11 - HSYNC
                pin18 - VSYNC
                pin20 - DTR (DATA TERMINAL READY)
                pin21 - RI

J12:            LINE AUDIO IN (RIGHT)  (from LD player)

J13:            LINE AUDIO IN (LEFT)   (from LD player)

J16: (COUNTER)  pin 1 - TO NEG. OF COIN COUNTER
                pin 2 - +12V
                pin 3 - +12V
                pin 4 - TO NEG. OF COIN COUNTER (SAME AS PIN 1)


RS232 null modem cable for the Sony LD player (terminal mode):

    LD player             PCB P3 

   pin 7 (GND)    TO    pin 1 (GND)
   pin 2 (TxD)    TO    pin 3 (RxD)
   pin 3 (RxD)    TO    pin 2 (TxD)

 / pin 4 (RTS) \      / pin 4 (RTS) \
 \ pin 5 (CTS) /      \ pin 5 (CTS) /

 / pin 6 (DSR) \      / pin 6 (DSR) \
 \ pin20 (DTR) /      \ pin20 (DTR) /

Connect each set of two pins shown inside slashes to each other. 
e.g., Connect pins 4 and 5 of the connector on the LD end together.


Note: On the original harness by Leland, pin 1 was used for GND on
the laserdisc end of the RS232 cable. Pin 1 is not connected to
anything inside the LD player, even though it is labeled as ground
in the Sony user manual. If pin 1 is used, the PCB will not be able
to control the LD player unless the audio cables are connected
between the LD player and the PCB. Use pin 7 on the LD end of the 
RS232 connector for ground.


Sony LDP-1450 Laserdisc Player DIP Switch Settings:

- Baud rate: (9600)   #1 - DOWN
                      #2 - DOWN
                      #3 - DOWN
                      #4 - UP

- Optional Function Selector:  Place all six DIP switches in the down position.
